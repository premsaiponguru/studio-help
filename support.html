Common questions

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. The whole game is inside the app and plays offline, with no sign-in. You only need the internet to buy something from the App Store.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, automatically. Deleting the app deletes the save, so keep a save code first if you want to keep your studio.

How do I move my studio to a new iPhone or iPad?

On the old device: Settings → Save code → Copy my code, and send the code to yourself (for example in Notes or Messages). On the new device: Settings → Load a code and paste it.

The Starter Pack and the Star Studio Pass also come back on the new device through Restore purchases, as long as you use the same Apple Account.

I bought something but didn't get it
  • Check that you are online, then close and reopen the game. Unfinished purchases are delivered again automatically.
  • For the Starter Pack or the Star Studio Pass: tap the 💎 gems at the top, then Restore purchases at the bottom of the Gem Shop.
  • Gem packs are used up when you receive them, so Apple cannot restore them. If gems are missing, e-mail us with the date of the purchase.
How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the purchase.

Can I stop purchases on my child's device?

Yes. In the iPhone or iPad Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases → In-app Purchases → Don't Allow. With Family Sharing you can also turn on Ask to Buy, so every purchase needs your approval. (The menu names can differ slightly between iOS versions.) The game itself always asks "Buy with real money?" before Apple's purchase sheet.

Is the game fair if I never pay?

Yes. Everything you need to progress can be earned by playing. Purchases are optional shortcuts and extras, each shows exactly what it gives, and nothing for sale is random. There are no ads.

Where do gift codes come from?

We post free codes on Instagram from time to time. Type one in Settings → Gift codes. Each code works once per studio. You never have to follow, like or share anything to use a code.

How do I play?

Walk with the stick at the bottom left (push it far to run, or tap the floor). Tap the big button at the bottom right next to a customer to serve them, collect coins and tidy up. Drag to turn the camera and pinch to zoom. Settings → Help shows the controls and can replay the tutorial.

The game feels slow or my device gets warm

Close other apps and give the first launch a little time to load the 3D shop. On smaller or older devices, and in Low Power Mode, the game automatically uses lighter graphics.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or newer. On iPhone the game plays in landscape; on iPad you can hold it either way.
