Hero of the Nine Lands · Nine lands. One hero. Every label broken.

A king labelled nine lands. One hero rides home to break every label.

Build your hometown back, lead your army, free nine lands and storm the villain king's fort. A story-led town builder you play with one finger: drag to ride, scoop up gold from the fields, stop on a round stone to build.

  • No ads

The story, in comics

Sixteen short comic scenes, drawn in the game's own style: the prologue, a meeting in every land, the fort striking back, the king's challenge and the finale. Skip any time, and watch them again in Story so far.

A hero and his people

A charming hometown hero, the witty Engineer of Glass City, a mother who wants you to eat first, a grandma who laughs at danger, a forest ranger, a retired colonel, a dance troupe and more. Friends you make ride along with you.

Nine lands, nine answers

River Farm Town, the Villas, Red Forest, Glass City, the Gold Mines, Sea Front, Star Plaza, Misty Hills and the Great Dam. Each grows from ruins to busy streets as you build, then the siege of the Villain's Fort.

Your army rides with you

Green stone: your army attacks. Blue stone: they come back to heal. Every land you free sends its guard to join you as veterans. Losing a wave is never game over.

Fair and friendly

  • Every land, building, story scene and land ride can be won by playing.
  • Optional purchases (gem packs, a one-time Starter Pack and a one-time Gold Pass) show exactly what they give. Nothing for sale is random, and nothing is a subscription.
  • About 10 hours of story, then NEW SAGA+ with more gold each time round. Your town keeps earning while you are away.
  • Portrait on iPhone, any way round on iPad.
