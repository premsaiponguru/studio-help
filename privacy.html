Privacy policy

Prettopia · Effective date: 15 October 2026

The short version

  • We collect no data. Nothing about you or how you play is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Prettopia is a game made by Prem Ponguru, an independent developer. In this policy "we" means Prem Ponguru. You can reach us at premsai.ponguru@gmail.com.

What the game keeps on your device

To remember your studio, the game saves your progress only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. This includes the names you type for yourself and your shop, your character's look, your level, coins, gems, clothes, decor, settings, and the photos in your in-game album.

This save is never uploaded. We cannot see it, and nobody else can either. If you delete the app, the save is deleted with it. You can also wipe it any time in Settings → Start over.

Things that only happen when you choose them

  • Save code. Settings → Save code copies your progress as a text code, so you can move it to another device. The code goes only where you paste it.
  • Saving a picture. When you tap "Save picture", iOS shows its share sheet. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks for permission to add the picture to your Photos. The game never reads your photo library.
  • Community link. Settings → Community opens Instagram outside the game (in the Instagram app or Safari). Instagram's own privacy policy applies there. Following is never required.
  • Gift codes. Codes are checked inside the game, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

In-app purchases

The game is free, and purchases are optional. Every purchase goes through Apple's App Store using the Apple Account on your device. Apple handles the payment under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your name, Apple Account, card or payment details.

The game only receives Apple's confirmation of which item was bought, and uses it on your device to give you that item. "Restore purchases" asks Apple which one-time items your account owns.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and no social media plug-ins. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone. It does not need an internet connection to play.

Apple gives every developer some general statistics, such as how many times an app was downloaded, and crash reports from people who chose to share them with developers in their iPhone or iPad settings. These come from Apple, are not linked to you, and we use them only to improve the game.

Children

Prettopia is a gentle game for everyone. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases need the approval of the person who owns the Apple Account, and parents can use Ask to Buy (Family Sharing) and Screen Time to approve or switch off purchases.

If you believe a child has e-mailed us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

If you e-mail us

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you include in your message. We use them only to reply to you. We do not share them or add you to any mailing list, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

Wherever you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the UK GDPR, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you can ask us what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Because the game sends us nothing, the only thing we could ever hold is an e-mail you sent us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If the game ever started to collect any data, we would update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
