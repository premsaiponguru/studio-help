Help & support

Goldride for iPhone and iPad

Contact us

Something not working, a purchase question, or an idea? Write to premsai.ponguru@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. It helps to say which device you use and what you were doing when it happened.

Playing

How do I move?

Drag anywhere on the screen to ride; the direction you drag is the direction you go. Lift your finger to stop. There is nothing else to press: everything happens by riding onto things.

How do I build?

Ride past the harvest fields to scoop up their gold into your ride. Then stop on a round stone: the coins fly in, the price counts down and the building pops up. Upgrade stones appear when you ride near a building. The glowing trail on the ground always points to your next goal.

How do the story comics work?

The prologue plays the first time you open the game, and a short meeting scene plays the first time you ride into each land. Tap to turn the page. To skip, tap Skip (top right): twice for a scene you have not seen yet, once for one you have. A scene never interrupts a fight or an open menu. Watch any scene again in The saga → Story so far.

How do I command my army?

Your soldiers ride with you on your own ground; the chip at the top left shows how many (★ = veterans) and what they are doing. Ride onto the green stone and they attack; ride onto the blue stone and they come back to heal. Every land you free sends its guard to join you as veterans.

Where are Settings?

The gear at the top of the round buttons on the right edge. On iPhone, once six or more buttons show, Settings, Legacy and the saga sit behind the ⋯ button. Settings has How to play, sound, haptics, camera zoom and your purchases.

How do I get a new ride?

Every land you open gives you a new ride. You keep all of them: stand on a ride stone at the Garage to swap. Colours for your rides are in Shop → Garage and cost gold you carry.

I lost a wave. Is my game over?

Never. Some buildings may be wrecked; rebuild them, upgrade your watchtowers and guards, and the next wave goes better. Your progress is never taken away.

Does the game need the internet?

No. The whole game is inside the app and plays offline. Only buying or restoring purchases needs a connection to Apple.

What happens while I'm away?

Your town keeps working. When you come back, a welcome-back card shows what your fields and carts collected (counted for up to 4 hours).

Purchases

Do I have to pay?

No. Goldride is free, with no ads. Every land, building and land ride can be won by playing, and gems are also earned free (level-ups, achievements, daily quests, chests and freed lands). Purchases are optional.

Where is the shop?

Tap the gem counter at the top of the screen, or the gem button in the column of round buttons on the right edge. Gem packs, the Starter Pack and the Gold Pass are in the Gems tab. Every item says exactly what it gives, and nothing for sale is random.

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Settings → Purchases → Restore (or Shop → Gems → Restore purchases). The Starter Pack and the Gold Pass come back. Gem packs are used up when bought, so Apple does not restore them.

I bought something but didn't get it.

Close the game completely and open it again: unfinished purchases are delivered on the next start. If it is a Starter Pack or Gold Pass, also try Restore. Still missing? E-mail us with the date of the purchase.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the purchase.

Can I stop a child from buying?

Yes. Every purchase first asks "Buy with real money?" and then Apple asks for approval. Parents can use Ask to Buy (Family Sharing) or switch off in-app purchases in Screen Time.

Your save

Where is my game saved?

Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud save, so deleting the app also deletes the town. Purchases of the Starter Pack and the Gold Pass belong to your Apple Account and can always be restored.

Can I start again?

Settings → Restart this town starts the town again and keeps your gems, premium rides and colours. Settings → Erase everything deletes the whole game on this device (purchased items stay yours and can be restored).

Which devices work?

iPhone (played upright, in portrait) and iPad (any way round).
